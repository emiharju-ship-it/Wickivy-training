2. Support & Moderation Guidelines

How we handle tickets, Discord chat, and Minecraft gameplay support with kindness.

Ticket Handling Standards

  • Claim open Discord tickets promptly and acknowledge the user within 5 minutes.
  • Always maintain a calm, empathetic, and friendly tone regardless of user frustration.
  • Confirm resolution before closing any ticket using: "Is there anything else I can help you with today?"

De-escalation & Soft Moderation

  • Use gentle reminders in general chat before taking official staff actions.
  • For minor rule breaks (caps, spam, mild arguments): issue a soft reminder in chat or DM.
  • Log all severe offenses (griefing, harassment, hate speech) directly in staff channels with proof/screenshots.
